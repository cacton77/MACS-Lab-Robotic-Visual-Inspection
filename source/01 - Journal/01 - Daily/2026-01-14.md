# 2026-01-14

We had formulated our admittance control problem as a sphere moving through a fluid governed by Stoke's Law. However, this resulted in the terminal rotational velocity being reached 10/3 times faster than the terminal linear velocity was reached. This caused the critical v = ωr relationship to break down immediately after the first motion of the sphere. By simply scaling the rotational drag coefficient by 3/10 we can preserve the v = ωr relationship. We are breaking Stoke's Law by setting
$$c_T = 2.4πμR³$$
But hey, this is our system!

Additionally, we forgot to consider the necessity of centripetal force, thus we will add a force in the z-axis 
$$F_c = \frac{mv^2}{r}$$
