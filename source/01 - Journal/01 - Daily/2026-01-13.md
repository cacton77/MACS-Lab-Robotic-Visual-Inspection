### [Drag Force: Stoke's Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stokes%27_law)

"In [fluid dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_dynamics "Fluid dynamics"), **Stokes' law** gives the frictional force – also called [drag force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_force "Drag force") – exerted on [spherical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere "Sphere") objects moving at very small [Reynolds numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reynolds_number "Reynolds number") in a [viscous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viscosity "Viscosity") [fluid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid "Fluid")"
#### Linear
$$\vec{F}_d = -6\pi\mu R\vec{v}$$
$$c_F = -6\pi\mu R$$
#### Rotational
$$\vec{\tau}_d = -8\pi\mu R^3\omega_R$$
### Dynamics
#### Point A
$$\sum \tau = \tau_A - \vec{F}_d$$
